# Lightspeed Email Marketing: Why Klaviyo Beats Mailchimp (With Proper Integration)

If you're a Lightspeed retailer looking for email marketing, you've probably considered three options:

1. **Lightspeed Marketing** - Native but limited
2. **Mailchimp** - Popular but clunky integration
3. **Klaviyo** - Powerful but no native Lightspeed integration

Most retailers either settle for Mailchimp or give up on proper email marketing entirely.

But there's a fourth option: **Klaviyo with proper Lightspeed integration.**

Here's why that's the move.

## Option 1: Lightspeed Marketing

**The good:**
- Native integration (obviously)
- Simple to set up
- Included in some Lightspeed plans

**The bad:**
- Limited automation capabilities
- Basic segmentation
- No advanced flows (abandoned cart recovery, browse abandonment, etc.)
- You outgrow it fast

**Verdict:** Fine for sending newsletters. Not a real email marketing platform.

## Option 2: Mailchimp

**The good:**
- Brand recognition
- Easy to use interface
- Has a Lightspeed integration (sort of)

**The bad:**
- Integration is limited (contact sync, not full purchase data)
- Automation is clunky compared to competitors
- Pricing gets expensive fast
- Not built for ecommerce/retail

**The real problem:** Mailchimp's Lightspeed integration syncs contacts but not detailed purchase history. You can't build "bought running shoes → recommend running socks" flows. You can't segment by purchase value or product category.

**Verdict:** Better than nothing, but you're not getting the full value of email marketing.

## Option 3: Klaviyo (Without Proper Integration)

**The good:**
- Best-in-class ecommerce email platform
- Powerful automation and flows
- Deep segmentation
- Built for driving revenue, not just sending emails

**The bad:**
- No native Lightspeed integration
- Requires manual CSV exports or janky Zapier setups
- Without integration, you lose most of what makes Klaviyo great

**Verdict:** Klaviyo is the best platform, but without proper data sync, you're driving a Ferrari in first gear.
