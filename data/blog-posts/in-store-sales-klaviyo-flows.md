# Why Your In-Store Sales Aren't Triggering Klaviyo Flows (And How to Fix It)

Your Shopify customer abandons a cart. Within an hour, they get an email. They complete a purchase. They get a thank you, a review request, product recommendations.

Your in-store customer walks in, spends $500, and walks out.

They get... nothing.

No thank you email. No review request. No loyalty program notification. No cross-sell recommendations. Nothing.

This is the omnichannel blindspot that's costing retailers thousands in repeat revenue.

## The Math on Missing In-Store Flows

Let's say you have 100 in-store customers per month spending an average of $150.

**Without post-purchase flows:**
- No review requests → Fewer reviews → Lower trust → Fewer new customers
- No cross-sell emails → Missed upsell revenue
- No win-back campaigns → Customers forget you exist

**With post-purchase flows (industry averages):**
- Post-purchase emails: 8-10% click rate
- Review request emails: 5-15% completion rate  
- Cross-sell recommendations: 10-30% of email revenue

If even 10% of your in-store customers make a repeat purchase from email → that's 10 extra sales × $150 = **$1,500/month** you're leaving on the table.

And that's conservative.

## Why This Happens

Klaviyo is built for ecommerce. Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce—they all have native integrations that push order data to Klaviyo in real-time.

Lightspeed Retail? No native integration.

Your POS knows who bought what. But that data stays trapped in Lightspeed. Klaviyo never sees it.

So your email flows can only trigger on online behavior. In-store is invisible.

## The Zapier Band-Aid

Some agencies try to bridge this with Zapier:

1. Sale happens in Lightspeed
2. Zap fires
3. Customer added to Klaviyo list
4. Flow triggers

**Problems:**
- You get customer info, not product data (can't do "bought X, recommend Y")
- Zaps break constantly (Lightspeed API is finicky)
- No historical data sync
- Expensive once you need multiple Zaps

It's better than nothing. But it's not a real solution.
