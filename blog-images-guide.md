# Blog Images Guide

This document contains AI image prompts and suggestions for each blog post.

## How to Generate Images

### Option 1: AI Image Generators (Recommended)
- **Leonardo.ai** (https://leonardo.ai) - Free tier available
- **Ideogram.ai** (https://ideogram.ai) - Free to use
- **ChatGPT DALL-E** - If you have ChatGPT Plus
- **Midjourney** - Best quality but requires subscription

### Option 2: Free Stock Photos
- **Unsplash** (https://unsplash.com)
- **Pexels** (https://pexels.com)

## Quick Instructions for Adding Images

Once you have the images:

1. Save images to: `public/blog-images/`
2. Update markdown files to include images like this:

```markdown
![Description of image](/blog-images/filename.png)
```

Example:
```markdown
## Why Integrate Lightspeed with Klaviyo?

![Lightspeed and Klaviyo integration flow diagram](/blog-images/integration-flow-diagram.png)

Lightspeed is one of the most popular POS systems...
```

---

## Recommended Image Specs

- **Format**: PNG (for transparency) or JPG
- **Width**: 1200px - 1600px
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 or 4:3
- **File Size**: Under 500KB (optimize with TinyPNG.com)
- **Style**: Consistent across all images - modern, clean, professional
- **Colors**: Use the green accent (#a3e635) to match your brand

---

## Alternative: Use Canva Templates

If you prefer not to use AI:

3. Customize with your brand colors
